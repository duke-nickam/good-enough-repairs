## Task card: Economy Balancer

**Goal:** Tune the economy so that pure buying loses money and smart swapping makes money. That gap is the whole game. Produce one balanced economy file for the game.

**Starting values (yours to change):** GDD numbers are starting values, not law. You may change starting credits (even to 0), part prices, ship payments, upkeep, scrap price, junkyard price and the difficulty ramp. Keep the GDD's relations: junkyard price is flat, above 10 and far below 150. Penalty price is new part price + 20%. Scrap is never worth more than a swap.

**Do not change:**
- Required levels: fighters 70%, freighters 60%
- 3 docking bays
- 10 inventory slots
- 7 days
- 1-3 weak parts per ship
- Quiet days have 5-10 ships
- Part prices stay fixed during a run

**QA Playtester will check:**
- Buyer bot goes bankrupt by about day 3 (in 90%+ of runs, median day 2-4)
- Swapper bot survives all 7 days (90%+ of runs) and stays slightly positive on the hardest day (day 7 net >= 0)
- A worst-case day 4 is recoverable: the swapper still survives the run in 75%+ of runs
- Releasing unrepaired ships never pays: releaser bot goes bankrupt in 90%+ of runs
- Filling one weak part costs more than the lowest ship payment

You have 3 rounds. If a target keeps failing, report which one and why.

## Director - decision

## Decision: ESCALATED

Three rounds ran and one target still fails. Four of five checks passed in round 3.

- **Failing target:** `buyer_bankrupt`. The buyer goes bankrupt in 99.3% of runs, but the median bankruptcy day is 6. The target is day 2-4.
- **Cause:** The buyer is roughly break-even on days 1-3. Making it fail earlier needs more cost or less starting cash. Either change squeezes the swapper, whose day-7 net is only +130.5 and whose worst-case day 4 only survives because of the starting credits.

**The designer must decide:**
- **Option A, loosen the target.** Accept a buyer median bankruptcy of day 5-6, since it still reliably goes broke. This is the least risky option, and the current file already meets everything else.
- **Option B, raise part prices.** Try QA's suggested `partPrice` of 180-220, or the softer 170-210. This may push the swapper's day 7 net below 0, and I'd expect it to.
- **Option C, change the design.** Add a mechanic that makes early days harder for buyers without touching the swapper, such as a bigger or earlier upfront cost. This is a GDD change, not a tuning change.

My recommendation is Option A.
